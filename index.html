About CHEEGERS

Chengdu Cheegers Machinery Manufacturing Co., Ltd. makes bulk material handling equipment, including rotary airlock valves, diverter valves, slide gate valves and magnetic separators.

We have over 60 years of practical experience in rotary airlock valve design and production. We work closely with global partners and offer custom solutions for different bulk material conveying needs.

Our CHEEGERS products are sold to more than 30 countries like Germany, UK, Italy, Canada and Southeast Asia. Our rotary airlock valves are precision built with good air sealing performance. We maintain strict quality control and reliable after-sales service, winning trust from customers worldwide.
