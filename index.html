Rotary Airlock Valve (Star Feeder)

Rotary airlock valve, also named star discharge valve / rotary feeder. It is core equipment for silo discharge, dust removal and pneumatic conveying. Our valves are available for normal pressure, negative pressure, positive pressure and heavy wear service.

  • Housing Material: Cast iron, SS304, SS316L, wear-resistant alloy
  • Sealing: Packing seal / Oil seal optional
  • Drive: Gear motor, variable speed available
  • High wear resistant model for abrasive powder
  • ISO9001 certified, CAD drawings offered
